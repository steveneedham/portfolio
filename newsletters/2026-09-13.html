Urban Insights

What I'm Watching

Columbus's ridership dipped while the network grew

The city's 2025 Bike Plus Annual Report puts Columbus shared bike and scooter trips at 812,577 for the year — down 17% from 977,000 in 2023, against a citywide target of +5% annually. Meanwhile the city laid 15.9 miles of new or under-construction bike lanes in the same period. More lane-miles didn't move ridership on its own; worth sitting with before assuming infrastructure is the whole answer.

Ridership   Columbus

One protected street, zero reported crashes

Also from the Bike Plus report: protecting East Broad Street cut excessive speeding (55+ in a 35) by 89% and total crashes by 50%, with zero reported bike or scooter crashes from May through November. One corridor, one intervention. That's the kind of result that should be showing up in every future project's before/after numbers, not just this one.
