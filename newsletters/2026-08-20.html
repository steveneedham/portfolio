Fleet operations update: mid-August snapshot

Spent the past two weeks analyzing rebalance patterns across the downtown core. Noticed a consistent 11am bottleneck on high-usage corridors — operators are hitting peak capacity before lunch, then dragging into afternoon. Simple fix: shifted one dedicated rebalancer to start 30 minutes earlier. Reduced mid-day shortage incidents by roughly 23% week-over-week.

Portfolio Work: In Progress

Columbus PantryMap — civic tech retrofit

Rebuilding the community food-access dashboard with live data pipelines. The original design was solid, but static — it couldn't respond when a pantry went offline or shifted hours. New version pulls real-time status from three public health data sources and flags operational gaps for the food-access team. Launch targeted for late September.

Operations dashboard — playbook automation

Started work on a centralized ops dashboard that turns field observations into structured playbooks. The idea: operators log a problem (vehicle down, zone underperforming, customer report) into a simple form, and the system surfaces similar past incidents with their resolutions. Reduces decision time and keeps knowledge from walking out the door when someone leaves.

What I'm Watching

Mobility demand patterns shifting with back-to-school

Mid-August always brings behavioral change — summer tourists dry up, students return, campus routes spike. This year we're seeing an earlier shift than past years, suggesting a compressed late-summer window. Strategic question: do we pre-stage inventory at campus hub locations, or wait for demand to materialize? Early staging costs more upfront but reduces shortage risk.

Demand Forecasting   Columbus

Downtown development: what it means for micro-mobility

Three major projects underway in the Arena District — new residential, mixed-use retail, a redesigned pedestrian plaza. More interesting than the projects themselves is the shifted foot traffic pattern they're creating. The current hub placement was designed for 2019 patterns; if these projects complete on schedule, we'll have a 15–20% traffic redistribution by Q1 2027. Worth mapping now.
